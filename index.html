Disaster Hazards & Safety Measures

Earthquake

Before: Secure furniture, prepare kit, identify safe spots.

During: Drop, cover, hold on, stay away from windows.

After: Check injuries, avoid damaged buildings.

Flood

Before: Monitor alerts, elevate valuables.

During: Move to higher ground, avoid water.

After: Avoid contaminated water.

Cyclone

Before: Secure windows, stock essentials.

During: Stay indoors, follow alerts.

After: Avoid debris, inspect property.

Tsunami

Before: Learn evacuation routes.

During: Move to higher ground.

After: Avoid flooded areas.

Fire

Before: Install alarms, plan escape routes.

During: Crawl low, exit building.

After: Call emergency, treat injuries.

Pandemic

Before: Vaccinate, stock medicines.

During: Wear mask, social distance.

After: Follow medical advice.

First Aid – Step by Step

🔍

⚡ Quick Action Required

In emergency situations, every second counts. Follow these procedures carefully and call emergency services if needed.

  • 🩹 Bleeding
    1. Apply direct pressure
    2. Elevate
    3. Bandage
    4. Seek hospital
  • 🔥 Burns
    1. Cool with water
    2. Cover loosely
    3. Do not burst blisters
    4. Seek medical help
  • ❤️ CPR
    1. Check responsiveness
    2. Call emergency
    3. 30 compressions + 2 breaths
    4. Repeat until help arrives
  • 🤕 Fracture
    1. Immobilize
    2. Support with splint
    3. Apply padding
    4. Hospital
  • ☠️ Poisoning
    1. Do not vomit
    2. Identify poison
    3. Call poison control
    4. Hospital immediately

🚑 Need Immediate Help?

If the situation is severe, don't hesitate to call emergency services.

    User Info / Safety Tips

    Emergency Kit

    • Water (3 days supply)
    • Non-perishable food (3 days)
    • Flashlight & batteries
    • First aid kit
    • Portable phone charger
    • Important documents (ID, insurance, medical)

    Evacuation Plan

    • Identify nearest shelters & safe spots
    • Know evacuation routes for your area
    • Keep family communication plan

    During a Disaster

    • Stay calm and follow official alerts
    • Avoid unnecessary travel
    • Check on neighbors if safe

    After a Disaster

    • Avoid unstable buildings or fallen wires
    • Follow official recovery instructions
    • Report hazards to local authorities

    Health & Hygiene

    • Wash hands frequently
    • Use masks during pandemics or smoke
    • Keep first aid supplies ready

    Useful Resources / Contacts

    • Local disaster management websites
    • Emergency helpline numbers (see Contact page)
    • Reliable news & weather alerts
